Why Specific Pages Beat General Pages

Propulsion's Solutions > Compliance page already names CMMC, HIPAA, SOC 2, ISO 27001, PCI-DSS, GDPR, CCPA, and NYDFS — but one page can't rank for a buyer researching “managed IT services,” a buyer researching “managed cybersecurity services,” and a buyer researching “compliance as a service” all at once, because Google and AI engines are trying to match a specific question to a specific answer, not a general one. This is also how AI search actually works: a single prompt like “best managed IT provider for a growing business” triggers several related sub-searches behind the scenes, and the engine assembles its answer from whichever pages address each piece most directly. The pages in the table to the right exist to give Google and AI systems one clear, specific answer for each buyer type instead of asking them to guess which part of a general page applies.

Integration Playbook

Every acquisition Propulsion brings into the network carries its own domain, its own local search equity, and its own customer relationships built over years. Folding a new business into the Propulsion brand without losing that value takes a deliberate process, done the same way every time. This is that process.

1
Plan the Timeline

A 60–90 day minimum window, set before any public change happens.

What we do

  • Full URL identification and redirect mapping for the acquired site
  • Assess existing local-pack value and page-level authority worth preserving
  • Set the announcement cadence with the business owner and local team
Every acquisition is different — a site with real rankings gets a more careful, staged approach than one with almost no footprint to protect.
2
Announce the Transition

Customers and local relationships hear about the change before search engines force the issue.

What we build

  • Pop-up alert on the old site introducing the new Propulsion name
  • Landing page on propulsiontech.com announcing the acquisition
  • Co-branded “powered by Propulsion” treatment during the transition window
The goal is a customer who already recognizes the new name by the time the old site disappears — not a surprise redirect.
3
Sunset & Consolidate

Once the transition window closes, the technical migration happens all at once — not gradually.

What we do

  • Full change of address filed with Google and Bing
  • 301 redirects mapped one-for-one to preserve ranking value and link equity
  • GBP and listing channels transferred and consolidated under the Propulsion brand
  • 30-day investigation of redirect traffic to confirm the migration is capturing value, not losing it
This is the same redirect and change-of-address discipline that let Nology Networks build the SEO equity it has today — the process protects what already works instead of starting over.
